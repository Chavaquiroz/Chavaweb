**OPERA** is an airline operations optimization project developed at Optimen for LATAM Airlines, focused on improving pilot and crew scheduling through mathematical optimization and software engineering. The project involved developing and refining optimization models to address complex operational constraints, improve scheduling efficiency, and reduce airline operating costs.

### Key Highlights
- **Airline Crew Optimization:** Designed and implemented constraint-based optimization algorithms for airline crew rostering and pairing at LATAM Airlines.
- **Operational Cost Reduction:** Contributed to optimization solutions associated with a reported 5% reduction in operational costs.
- **Complex Constraint Modeling:** Modeled real-world airline scheduling problems involving operational requirements, crew assignments, and feasibility constraints.
- **Scalable Optimization Solutions:** Developed and evaluated algorithms for complex airline operations, balancing solution quality with computational efficiency.
- **Industry Applications:** Applied mathematical optimization and software development techniques to real-world aviation planning challenges.

### Tech Stack & Optimization
- **Optimization Algorithms (`Java + Timefold`):** Implementing constraint-based optimization models to generate and improve feasible airline crew schedules.
- **Airline Operations (`Rave + Python`):** Working with specialized airline planning technologies and programming tools to support operational optimization workflows.
- **Cloud Infrastructure (`AWS`):** Leveraging cloud technologies within the development and execution environment for aviation software solutions.
- **Development Environment (`IntelliJ IDEA`):** Developing, testing, and maintaining Java-based optimization software.
- **Version Control (`Git`):** Managing code changes and collaborating on software development within an industry engineering workflow.
