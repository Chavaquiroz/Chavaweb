**A Random Space Game** is a small, self-contained space-themed platformer developed as an interactive portfolio project to showcase game development skills. Players take control of an astronaut aboard a spaceship overrun by hostile aliens, fight their way through the vessel, and face a final boss to complete the mission. The game features a straightforward objective and a completion timer, offering visitors a hands-on demonstration of gameplay programming and independent game development.

### Key Highlights
- **Platformer Gameplay:** Implemented core movement, jumping, combat, and enemy encounters in a 2D space environment.
- **Alien Combat:** Designed encounters where players must eliminate hostile aliens throughout a spaceship.
- **Final Boss Encounter:** Created a final challenge that marks the culmination of the player's mission.
- **Completion Timer:** Tracks the time taken to finish the game, giving players a simple way to measure their performance.
- **Interactive Portfolio Project:** Developed a playable experience that allows visitors to explore practical game development skills directly through a personal website.

### Tech Stack & Development
- **Game Engine (`Godot`):** Implementing player controls, platforming mechanics, combat systems, enemy behavior, and game progression.
- **Pixel Art (`Aseprite`):** Creating and editing sprites and visual assets for the astronaut, aliens, and spaceship environment.
- **Audio Production (`LMMS`):** Producing music and audio elements to enhance the space-themed atmosphere.
- **Cross-Platform Development (`Android`):** Exploring platform-specific build and deployment workflows.
- **Development Workflow (`Bash`):** Using command-line tools to support development and project management.
