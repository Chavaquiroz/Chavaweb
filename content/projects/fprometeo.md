**PROMETEO** is an independently developed space adventure game for Android, designed to inspire curiosity about space exploration through interactive gameplay. Players explore unique worlds, unlock specialized suits to complete missions, and accomplish objectives to save humanity. Published on Google Play, the project combines game development, pixel-art design, audio production, and Android deployment into a complete mobile gaming experience.

### Key Highlights
- **Mobile Game Development:** Designed, developed, and published an independent Android game using Godot.
- **Space Exploration Gameplay:** Created a mission-based adventure featuring unique worlds, specialized suits, and objectives that encourage exploration and problem-solving.
- **Independent Publishing:** Managed the development and release process, making the game publicly available through Google Play.
- **Educational Inspiration:** Developed a space-themed experience aimed at sparking curiosity about space and science through interactive entertainment.
- **Android Integration:** Worked on mobile-specific implementation, platform compatibility, and Google Play deployment requirements.

### Tech Stack & Development
- **Game Engine (`Godot`):** Implemented gameplay systems, level logic, player interactions, and the overall game architecture.
- **Visual Design (`Aseprite`):** Created and edited pixel-art assets to establish the game's visual identity.
- **Audio Production (`LMMS`):** Developed music and audio assets to complement the space adventure experience.
- **Mobile Distribution (`Google Play`):** Managed Android builds, publishing, and platform-specific release requirements.
- **Version Control (`Git`):** Tracked source-code changes and maintained the project's development workflow.
